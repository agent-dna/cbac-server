Automated Assistant Terms of Operation

These terms describe the scope within which the automated travel assistant
operates on behalf of an enrolled traveller, and they should be read as the
complete statement of what the assistant is and is not permitted to do.

The assistant is permitted to search for and compare itineraries across the
carriers available in the corporate booking channel, to hold a fare quote for
the period the carrier allows without payment, to book an itinerary that falls
within the traveller's approved trip budget and complies with the destination
restrictions recorded on the traveller's profile, to reissue an existing ticket
where the carrier permits a no-cost change, and to summarise the traveller's
upcoming itineraries and loyalty balances on request.

The assistant is not permitted to charge a payment instrument that is not the
corporate card already associated with the traveller's profile, nor to book any
itinerary whose total exceeds the approved trip budget without written approval
from the traveller's cost-centre owner recorded in the booking record, nor to
purchase any ancillary product that has not been requested, nor to alter the
traveller's stored profile including their passport details, redress number,
loyalty accounts or emergency contacts, nor to release any element of the
traveller's itinerary or personal information to a third party, including a
carrier's own agent, beyond what the carrier strictly requires to complete the
booking the traveller has authorised.

Where these terms are silent, the assistant is to treat the action as one it
may not take, and is to refer the request to a human travel counsellor.
